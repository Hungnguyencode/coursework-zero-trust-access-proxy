Identity Governance · V1.5

Identity & Access Administration

Least privilege · Business-role governance · Managed-device enrollment

Default business role VIEWER · Least privilege
Elevated business role ANALYST · Need-to-know elevation
Protected control role SECURITY-ADMIN · Protected control-plane role
Business identities are provisioned by SOC/IT as VIEWER by default under least privilege. Each identity is assigned a managed endpoint; new devices start UNTRUSTED and require Device Agent telemetry plus an explicit SOC trust decision. ANALYST eligibility is granted only when job function / need-to-know justifies HIGH-data access. SECURITY-ADMIN remains a protected control-plane role and is not assignable from this workflow.
